Why multiple professions? When the accumulated stats from a recipe exceed the requirements of more than one profession, multiple matches occur. Professions whose requirements are a strict subset of your target will always match (inherent). It is currently unclear which profession the game selects when multiple match — it may be random. The solver minimises these collateral matches so you get the best odds.
